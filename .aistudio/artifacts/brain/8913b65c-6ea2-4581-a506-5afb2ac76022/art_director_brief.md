# Atrium OS — Creative & Strategic Design Brief for the Art Director

This document answers the six foundational questions posed by the Art Director. It articulates the exact product truth, user psychographics, Algerian operational context, visual guidelines, and brand constitution of **Atrium ERP & CRM (Atrium OS)**.

---

## 1. The Product and Its Truth

### What’s the app called, and what does it do in one sentence if you said it to a friend, not a client?
> **Atrium OS** is the operating system for your business that runs your cash desk, inventory, client files, invoices, and team from a single screen—built specifically for how commerce actually happens on the ground in Algeria.

### What is the real pain your users feel before they find you?
Before Atrium, business owners live in **perpetual operational anxiety**:
- **The Blind Ledger**: They work 14 hours a day, handle millions of Dinars, but at midnight they still cannot answer: *"Did I actually make a profit today, or did supplier bills eat it all?"*
- **Siloed Chaos**: Sales are rung up on a tactile calculator; stock levels are estimated by eye; client tabs are scribbled on paper notebooks (*carnets de crédit*); delivery notes (*bons de livraison*) are lost between the driver and the warehouse; and project deadlines vanish in WhatsApp audio notes.
- **The Settlement Gap**: Disconnected Algerian payment realities—cash handovers, postal check mandates (*Chèques CCP SFP 01*), BaridiMob screenshots, and delayed bank transfers (*virements BNA/BEA/CPA*) that never reconcile with tax invoices (*TVA & Timbre Fiscal*).

### What’s the feeling you want them to have after onboarding: relief, control, pride, speed, calm?
**Sovereign Control and Deep Relief.**
The sensation of stepping out of a sandstorm into an architected courtyard: the noise stops, the numbers line up, every centime has an address, and the business owner transitions from a firefighter into an architect.

### If your product were a person in the room, who would it be?
**The Unflappable Chief Operating Officer and Trusted Right-Hand.**
Not an academic consultant with slide decks, and not a rigid tax bureaucrat. A sharp, composed partner who stands beside you on the warehouse floor or behind the counter: knows the Algerian commercial code by heart, respects cash flow discipline, never loses composure when the showroom is packed, and speaks in plain, direct language.

## 2. Users and Context

### Who is the person looking at these images?
- **Primary Viewer**: The **Founder / Business Owner / Managing Director** (*Gérant, Commerçant, Directeur de Clinique, Patron d’Agence*). They make the purchase decision, hold personal liability for the cash and the books, and set the operational rhythm.
- **Secondary Viewers**: Department heads (Operations Manager, Head Cashier, Project Lead, Head of Inventory) and front-line staff who need reassurance that the software won't slow down their daily shifts.

### Which industries or business types use it most? Where are they located?
- **Core Verticals**: 16 dedicated verticals covering **160+ specialized Algerian trades**:
  1. *Retail & POS*: Superettes, boutiques, electronics, pharmacies, beauty bars.
  2. *Healthcare & Clinical*: Dental practices, specialist medical clinics, optics, labs.
  3. *B2B Trading & Distribution*: Wholesale (*commerce de gros*), food distributors, construction materials, spare parts.
  4. *Professional Services & Studios*: Digital agencies, printing houses (*imprimeries*), architecture studios, law firms.
  5. *Personal Care & Wellness*: Spas, hair salons, fitness clubs with turnstile check-ins.
  6. *Light Manufacturing & Crafts*: Furniture makers, bakeries, textile workshops, packaging units.
- **Geographic Reality**: Primarily **Algeria (all 58 wilayas)** and the wider **North African / MENA region**, with international suppliers and diaspora-led ventures.

### What language and direction will the interface be?
- **Trilingual First-Class Support**:
  - **French (LTR)**: Preferred by commercial trade, corporate finance, medical clinics, and creative agencies.
  - **Arabic (RTL)**: Full native layout mirroring (not just translated strings)—composition, gaze paths, progress micro-dashes, and visual weights invert naturally. Widely used across retail, interior wilayas, and public administration documents.
  - **English (LTR)**: Tech studios, startups, and international exporters.
- **Visual Composition Impact**:
  - In LTR (French/English), the human eye moves from the question card on the left to the live adapting device monolith on the right.
  - In RTL (Arabic), the gaze enters from the right question card and resolves into the visual system on the left.
  - Imagery must maintain balanced focal points that remain compelling in both orientations without clipping primary visual anchors.

### Are they tech-comfortable, or intimidated by software?
**Pragmatic and Wary of Bloat.**
Many have been burned by overly complex Western enterprise suites (SAP, Odoo, Salesforce) that required months of setup and consultant fees, or by fragile pirated spreadsheets.
- **Design Mandate**: Imagery must **reassure through concrete competence**. Avoid frivolous sci-fi abstractions, cartoonish 3D bobbleheads, or vague "AI glow." The visual language should communicate solid engineering, instant usability, and respectful craftsmanship.

## 3. The Onboarding Itself

### How many onboarding screens or steps are there, and what does each one say?
The onboarding is structured as a **focused 9-step conversational workstation (Steps 0 through 8)**, followed by instant workspace launch:

| Step | Focus | Screen Header & Purpose | User Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | **Identity & Account** | *"Créez votre compte administrateur"* / *"أنشئ حساب المسؤول الرئيسي"* | Google 1-Click SSO or Work Email + Password via proprietary floating-notch `LabelInput`. |
| **1** | **Primary Friction** | *"Quel défi opérationnel souhaitez-vous résoudre ?"* | Single-tap selection of core pain point (scheduling, inventory leaks, unbilled hours, CCP checks, paper queues, B2B tax math). |
| **2** | **Feature Blueprint** | *"Que souhaitez-vous accomplir avec Atrium ?"* | Interactive operational blueprint modal showing the exact mechanisms activated to solve the challenge. |
| **3** | **Daily Workflow** | *"Comment vos clients interagissent-ils au quotidien ?"* | Defines front-desk terminals (Touch POS, appointments, turnstile barcode, design milestone BAT, patient queue, wholesale dispatch). |
| **4** | **Team Scale** | *"Quelle est la taille de votre équipe ?"* | Configures permission gates and roles (Solo, Small 2–5, Growth 6–20, Enterprise 20+). |
| **5** | **Settlement Flow** | *"Comment encaissez-vous vos recettes ?"* | Direct alignment with payment reality (POS Cash/BaridiMob, B2B TVA Facture, Postal CCP SFP 01 slips, Monthly Retainers). |
| **6** | **Trade Catalog** | *"Quelle est votre activité précise ?"* | Searchable directory of 160+ Algerian business trades with category filters. |
| **7** | **Discovery Channel** | *"Comment avez-vous entendu parler d’Atrium ?"* | Referral origin (LinkedIn, Instagram, word-of-mouth, press, ecosystem partners). |
| **8** | **Workspace & Plan** | *"Finalisez votre espace & choisissez votre forfait"* | Workspace naming and phone via `LabelInput`, 4-tier plan switcher (Free Web, Starter, Pro, Enterprise), lifetime voucher activation, or official license request modal. |

### Visual system across steps: one image per step, or an evolving system?
**A Single Evolving Visual System (The Live Adapting Monolith).**
Rather than disjointed static marketing illustrations, the right side of the screen features a live, responsive digital workstation that **mutates in real time** as the user makes decisions:
- Select *POS Retail* $\rightarrow$ The preview renders a tactile cash desk with BaridiMob, CIB cards, and Dinar balances.
- Select *Medical Practice* $\rightarrow$ The preview shifts to patient triage, electronic prescription pads, and waiting queue radar.
- Select *Creative Agency* $\rightarrow$ The preview displays sprint milestones, real-time billable stopwatches, and BAT client approval stamps.
- Select *B2B Trading* $\rightarrow$ The preview surfaces delivery manifests (*BL*), stamp duty calculations, and multi-depot stock counters.

### Where do the images sit? Sizes and aspect ratios?
- **Layout Architecture**: Strict **two-column viewport-locked workstation canvas** (`h-screen max-h-screen overflow-hidden`, zero document scrollbar).
- **Left Column (7 cols on lg)**: Holds the active question card, interactive choices, floating-notch inputs, and step navigation.
- **Right Column (5 cols on lg, hidden on mobile)**: A dedicated 440px wide showcase card containing the dark-mode Atrium Monolith, dynamic wilaya/entity badge, match confidence meter, and live vertical screen snapshot.
- **Cinematic Monolith Intro**: A centered full-viewport opening sequence that seamlessly dissolves into the workstation canvas in under 600ms without layout jumps.

### Room for text:
Yes. Every screen maintains strict typographical hierarchy with crisp spacing:
- Header: 24px bold title (`leading-snug`).
- Subtext: 13px muted zinc subheadline.
- Card padding and input heights (`h-10` to `h-11`) are calibrated so the layout never requires a scrollbar on standard laptop viewports ($1366\times768$ and $1920\times1080$).

## 4. Brand Constitution

### Logo, Name, Colors, and Typography

#### 1. The Name
**ATRIUM** (Derived from classical architecture: the open central court that serves as the heart and light-source of the entire building; in our software, the central hub that unites all operational functions).

#### 2. The Logo Emblem: The Five Cubes Mark
- **Shape & Form**: Five isometric geometric cubes arranged in an interlocking, modular architectural elevation.
- **Symbolism**:
  - The cubes represent modular business pillars: Sales, Inventory, Customers, Finance, and Human Capital.
  - The interlocking structure represents structural integrity and sovereign balance—no single cube can collapse without warning.
  - Geometric, clean, and architectonic; zero organic fluff.

#### 3. Color Palette
- **Primary Brand Accent**: **Warm Terracotta / Desert Copper**
  - Hex: `#C4785A`
  - Hover/Active: `#B3684A` / `#A0563B`
  - Ambient Glow: `rgba(196,120,90,0.25)`
  - *Cultural Meaning*: Evokes traditional Mediterranean and Saharan rammed-earth architecture (*ksour*, clay pottery, and baked tile), warm hospitality, and resilient endurance.
- **Surface & Dark Canvas**: **Obsidian & Deep Carbon**
  - Background: `#0C0C0E`
  - Container Surfaces: `#141416`, `#18181B`, `#1F1D1B`, `#221F1C`
  - Borders: `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)` to `rgba(255,255,255,0.15)`
- **Fiscal & Data Accents**:
  - Verified / Active: Emerald Green (`#10B981`, `#059669`)
  - Alerts / Timbre: Amber Coral (`#F59E0B`)
  - Typography: Crisp Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for primary headings, Warm Neutral (`#A1A1AA`) for secondary text.

#### 4. Typography
- **Latin Headings & UI**: Inter / SF Pro Display — ultra-clean, legible, industrial precision.
- **Arabic Typography**: Cairo / IBM Plex Sans Arabic — weighted, contemporary, without ornate calligraphic swashes that clutter dense software interfaces.
- **Financial & Code Numerals**: JetBrains Mono / Space Mono for currency amounts, voucher keys, and tax identification numbers (*NIF, NIS, RC, CCP*).

### Three Adjectives that Describe the Brand Today
1. **Architectural**: Clean-lined, proportioned, solid, built to bear heavy operational loads.
2. **Sovereign**: Puts full data ownership, perpetual licensing, and financial autonomy into the business owner's hands.
3. **Grounded**: Deeply authentic to the Algerian street, the real cash drawer, and national commercial statutes.

### Three Adjectives the Brand Must Never Feel Like
1. **Generic Corporate Slop**: No bland tech-blue gradients, fake stock photography of models shaking hands in glass towers, or soulless SaaS clichés.
2. **Toy-Like / Frivolous**: No neon bubblegum buttons, childish 3D emoji characters, or gamified confetti that insults serious business people.
3. **Foreign / Disconnected**: Must never feel like an unadapted translation of an American or European app that doesn't understand cash on delivery (*COD*), *CCP* slips, or the Algerian Dinar.

### Competitors & Visual Differentiation
- **The Competitor Landscape**:
  - *Traditional Desktop Legacy Software (PC Compta, PC Paie, Dlg)*: Dated Windows 95 aesthetics, grey tables, complex install keys, and zero mobile responsiveness.
  - *Western SaaS (Odoo, Zoho, QuickBooks)*: Bloated blue/purple dashboards, subscription traps in Euros/Dollars, credit card demands, and zero comprehension of Algerian postal checks or local commercial tax practices.
- **Our Visual Moat**:
  - Premium **dark-room studio atmosphere** with warm terracotta copper illumination.
  - Tactile, precise UI components: floating notch `LabelInput` fields, isometric micro-renderings, and instant trilingual parity.

## 5. The Art Director’s Core Questions

### What is the one message that must land?
> **"Your business, governed in one place."**

If the owner remembers only one conviction as they exit onboarding:
*"I am no longer guessing. Atrium has organized my real-world business into an unshakeable system, and I have complete command over my enterprise."*

### What are the two things that currently live apart that we bring together?
> **The chaotic ground-truth of the Algerian marketplace — and the pristine digital ledger of financial sovereignty.**

- On one side lives the **unfiltered physical reality**: the morning rush of cash in the drawer, the driver waiting with a delivery paper, the customer asking for credit on WhatsApp, the postal clerk stamping a CCP receipt, and the shelf of stock that needs reordering.
- On the other side lives the **owner’s need for clarity**: audited margins, reliable tax documents, accountable staff, and peace of mind.

Atrium collapses this tension. It does not force the merchant to abandon the Algerian way of doing business; it wraps that reality in world-class software architecture so they never lose a Dinar, an hour, or an opportunity again.
